1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This plan describes the design, build and operationalization of a "Customer 360"
data product for a global pharmaceutical company ("PharmaCo"). In pharma the
"customer" is not a single role: it spans Healthcare Professionals (HCPs),
Healthcare Organizations (HCOs), Patients (pseudonymized), and Payers. Customer
360 unifies engagement, prescribing, medical inquiry, sample distribution,
consent, and compliance data into a single governed, reusable data product.

The product is built on the Data Product Portal (Dataminded / Conveyor) as the
runtime/operational tool, and described using the Linux Foundation Open Data
Product specifications: ODPS (product), ODPC (catalog), ODPG (graph), and ODPV
(vocabulary). The Portal manages platform resources via Terraform; the ODP*
files travel with the product through Git, marketplaces and AI-assisted
discovery.

KEY OUTCOMES
  - +15% commercial productivity via next-best-action (NBA) for field reps
  - 100% Sunshine Act / EFPIA-compliant HCP transfer-of-value reporting
  - Sub-24h reach for adverse-event signal correlation
  - 360-degree HCP profile available to Sales, Medical, Marketing, Compliance


2. BUSINESS CONTEXT AND DRIVERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Why Customer 360 in pharma is different
  - The "customer" graph is multi-entity: HCP <-> HCO affiliations are many-to-
    many and change frequently.
  - Prescription data (Rx) is purchased from third parties (IQVIA, Symphony,
    Komodo) and must be reconciled with internal CRM/engagement data.
  - Patient data is highly regulated (HIPAA in the US, GDPR in the EU). It must
    be pseudonymized at ingestion, and the linkage key must be siloed.
  - Regulatory transparency: every transfer of value to an HCP (meals, samples,
    speaker fees) must be reported (US Sunshine Act, EFPIA Disclosure Code).
  - Pharmacovigilance: signals related to product safety must be detectable and
    auditable.

2.2 Business drivers
  - Commercial: target the right HCPs with the right message at the right time.
  - Medical: support MSL field activity with a complete view of KOL engagement.
  - Compliance: maintain a single source of truth for ToV reporting and consent.
  - Legal/Privacy: provable lineage and access control for every patient record.


3. SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 In scope (v1.0)
  - HCP master (US and EU5: France, Germany, Italy, Spain, UK)
  - HCO master and HCP-HCO affiliations
  - Engagement events: rep calls, emails, web, events, samples, ToV
  - Prescription / dispensed claims (Rx) from licensed third-party feed
  - Medical Information inquiries and adverse-event linkage (read-only)
  - Consent records and channel preferences
  - Identity resolution: master person ID + golden record

3.2 Out of scope (v1.0, deferred to v1.1+)
  - Direct-to-patient identifiable data
  - Real-world evidence (RWE) cohorts
  - Clinical trial subject data
  - Asia-Pacific markets


4. ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Runtime stack
  - Cloud: AWS (initial); Databricks (analytics); Snowflake (consumption)
  - Storage: S3 raw / bronze / silver / gold; Iceberg tables
  - Compute: Glue + Databricks notebooks; Athena for ad-hoc
  - Orchestration: Airflow
  - Identity: AWS IAM, Databricks Unity Catalog, Snowflake roles

4.2 Governance stack
  - Data Product Portal: data product registry, ownership, access requests
  - ODPS: machine-readable product description (this artifact, per product)
  - ODPC: portfolio catalog (Customer 360 lives alongside other products)
  - ODPG: graph of relationships across products, use cases, KPIs, policies
  - ODPV: pharma-specific vocabulary extension (terms like HCP, HCO, ToV)
  - Collibra: business glossary + lineage; ODPS exported to Collibra nightly
  - OneTrust: consent system of record; consent IDs join into the product

4.3 Integration pattern (Portal <-> ODPS)
  The Portal is the source of truth for operational resources (IAM, S3
  prefixes, Glue databases, Unity grants). A CI/CD exporter reads the Portal
  API on every change and emits one odps.yaml per data product to a Git
  repository. The Git repo also stores ODPC catalog files, the ODPG graph, and
  ODPV vocabulary extensions. ODPS files feed Collibra, the internal data
  marketplace, and AI-assistant retrieval (GraphRAG over ODPG).

    Portal (runtime)  -->  Exporter (CI/CD)  -->  Git (odps.yaml, odpc.yaml,
                                                  odpg.yaml, odpv-pharma.yaml)
                                                       |
                                                       v
                                            Collibra | Marketplace | AI Agents

6. COMMON DATA MODEL (CDM) - 14 TABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The CDM is organized in three layers and groups. See cdm/tables.md for full
column specs. Tables:

  HUB (master/reference)
    1.  dim_hcp                     Healthcare professional master
    2.  dim_hco                     Healthcare organization master
    3.  dim_hcp_hco_affiliation     HCP-HCO membership over time (SCD-2)
    4.  dim_product                 Pharma product master (drug/SKU)
    5.  dim_geography               Country / region / territory hierarchy
    6.  dim_consent                 Consent records, channel-level, OneTrust ID

  ENGAGEMENT (interactions)
    7.  fact_engagement             Calls, emails, web, events (unified)
    8.  fact_sample_disbursement    Drug samples given (Sunshine Act source)
    9.  fact_transfer_of_value      Meals, fees, travel - ToV reporting
   10.  fact_medical_inquiry        Med-info questions and responses

  COMMERCIAL (outcomes)
   11.  fact_prescription           Rx claims (third-party, restricted)
   12.  fact_segmentation           Decile/segment per HCP per product
   13.  fact_nba_recommendation     Next-Best-Action scores per HCP
   14.  fact_adverse_event_link     Link between engagement and PV cases


7. ACCESS, SECURITY AND GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Access scopes (enforced by the Portal via IAM and Unity grants)
  - "C360_READ_COMMERCIAL": Sales ops, brand teams. Excludes adverse-event
    linkage and medical inquiry free text.
  - "C360_READ_MEDICAL":    Medical Affairs, MSL ops. Adds inquiry text.
  - "C360_READ_COMPLIANCE": Compliance, internal audit. Adds full ToV ledger.
  - "C360_READ_PV":         Pharmacovigilance ops only. Adds AE linkage.
  - All access requires a documented use case in ODPC + steward approval.

7.2 Identity resolution
  - Primary HCP key: IQVIA OneKey ID (where available) fallback to NPI (US),
    RPPS (France), GMC (UK), then internal ID.
  - Patient: SHA-256 of (last name + DoB + zip) salted per data center.

7.3 Consent
  - Every engagement-channel record carries the OneTrust consent ID at the
    time of interaction. Engagement is filterable by consent state.

7.4 Regulatory and privacy controls
  - GDPR: EU records pinned to eu-west-1; right-to-be-forgotten propagates
    via Portal-orchestrated job.
  - HIPAA: Patient layer uses a separate Glue database and IAM boundary.
  - Sunshine Act / EFPIA: fact_transfer_of_value frozen monthly and signed.

7.5 Data quality (declarative, executable in Soda)
  - dim_hcp.npi: uniqueness 100%, format /^\d{10}$/, freshness <=24h
  - dim_consent.opt_in_status: completeness 100%
  - fact_prescription: latency from source <= 7 days, p95
